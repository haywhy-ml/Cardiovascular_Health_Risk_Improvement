Exploratory data analysis

What the data revealed

These charts come directly from the project notebook. They describe patterns in the survey data and should not be interpreted as proof that one factor causes heart disease.

01 · Demographics

Risk increases with age for both sexes

Male respondents show a higher observed heart disease rate in every age band, reaching 29.0% in the 80+ group.

02 · Self-rated health

A strong health gradient

Observed heart disease rises from about 2% among respondents reporting excellent health to nearly 32% among those reporting poor health.

03 · Comorbidities

Risk compounds as conditions accumulate

The observed rate rises steadily with the engineered comorbidity count, from roughly 3% with none to about 40% with all five.

04 · Model evaluation

The ranking works, but threshold choice matters

Average precision reaches 0.302. Lowering the decision threshold to 0.0924 increases positive recall from 7% to approximately 80%.
